# 🩺 MediRAG: Dr. Insight

An AI medical assistant that answers health questions from trusted sources (Gale Encyclopedia
of Medicine, OpenStax Pharmacology, MedlinePlus and WHO fact sheets) and shows where each answer
came from.

- Ask in English, Hindi or 8 other Indian languages, by typing or speaking
- Upload a lab report or photo and ask about it
- Guided symptom checker
- Emergency and crisis detection with Indian helpline numbers (112, 108, Tele-MANAS 14416)

> ⚠️ For learning and demonstration only. **Not** medical advice. In an emergency call 112.
>
> This free demo restarts after periods of inactivity, which clears saved chats and uploads.
> The first visit after a restart can take a few minutes while the models load.
